## What generalises
1. **A near-match caller whose prologue saves more callee-saved registers (or $f20/$f22) than its body uses is
   missing IPA context, not source.** Find the internal callee that uses them unsaved (here func_800F0914: s0-s8 and
   $f20/$f22 with no saves) and put a genuine reconstruction of it in the group as context. A diagnostic stand-in
   with the same clobber set is a quick way to prove the point before writing the real body.
2. **Per-register colour costs show call clobbers.** In `p1cost`, s0-s5 = 49-50 vs s6-s8 = 30 meant a callee clobbers
   s0-s5 inside that web's range; a loop counter shared by two loops was one web — two variables fixed the order.
3. **cfe gives `?:` results a frame slot** like a named local (two FLOOR ternaries = two bottom named slots).
4. `install.sh` takes the scratch dir FIRST: `install.sh ~/…/w15f --reuse ~/…/wtk`. With `--reuse` first it built
   into `~/--reuse` on the builder (removed again).
5. While the coordinator had src/blob mid-edit, a `git worktree` of HEAD (plus copies of build/blob_layout.json and
   build/game_code.bin) gave a stable unit to score against; the worktree has been removed.
