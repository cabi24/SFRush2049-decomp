- `tools/fx.py NAME`: retail disassembly with each jal annotated (N64 name, matched flag, w36n/w38a MAT3 identity) and the

## What generalises
1. **GC empty stubs and argument mismatches mark N64 platform bodies.** Several GC functions are left as stubs, or keep a
   call-site signature that disagrees with their own definition. In each case the N64 body survives behind it:
   - `static void LoadFrontEndOverlay(); /* called with an argument */` is a 4-byte stub; on N64 it is the overlay
     loader display_enable.
   - `PS_ReadPartialFile` is defined with 1 argument but called with 3.
   - `PS_PakServer(void)` is called with 2 arguments.
   - Erased `BGLoadWaveMain` and `MBOX_FindPalette_WORK` are real N64 functions.
   - `_GetCmd`'s `*(short*)cmd = -1` exists to tell commands from scheduler messages.

   Before declaring a function N64-only, grep the GC/DWARF tables for stripped or erased names and stub bodies in the
   file of its neighbours.
2. **The Dreamcast tree keeps N64-era statements that GC dropped. Prefer it where it is exact or close:**
   - initGameState's 13-font preload.
   - `LOCK()`/`UNLOCK()` around audio commands (N64: the 80142728 semaphore).
   - `SaveServer(0,0)` / `SaveFlush(1)`.
   - endDemoGame's slot loop.
   - The end-scene/begin-scene gfx statics.

   DC demo.c, model.c, gfx.c, TB_text.c and waves.c `_ReadWave` are exact units.
3. **The N64 runs as threads** (main-thread message pump, game thread, model/controller thread, audio-manager thread,
   BG-wave thread), and GC flattened them into per-frame calls. Thread entries come from the static `game_init` and
   from `AudioInit`'s `osCreateThread`. Inside each entry the GC per-tick body survives intact: ModelThread,
   `_ProcessCommand`, `_PeriodicMaintenance`.
4. **Unsaved-callee-register pairs** (IPA callees that use s-registers their caller saves) recur on the frontier:
   - func_80098FB8 with entity_ai_pathfind
   - _RemovePak with PS_PakServer
   - playDemoGame with updateDemoGame
   - LoadFrontEndOverlay with updateGameState
   - `_ReadWave` with its s8 parameter in BGLoadWaveMain
   - `_SetupFontData` with its t0 parameter in TB_GetFontHeight

   Match these pairs together, never alone.
5. **Unreferenced kept globals**: world_effect_update (InitGraphics) and object_bytes23_sum (TB_GetFontHeight) have
   no reference in the game image, either overlay or the static code. Their bodies also appear inlined in matched
   callers (InitSystem's initsystem.c, TB_DrawText), and those matched copies give the statement shape.
6. Observation (lead): the **game image** follows IPA post-order in the regions checked here too:
   - func_80098FB8's callee subtree (camera_transform .. _ProcessEmitters) sits directly before it.
   - SetupCarColor's callees (func_800B0EA0, stub 800B0F60, MBOX_FindPalette_WORK) sit before it.
   - The demo.c stubs sit before playDemoGame / updateDemoGame.

   The w38b stub rule may therefore predict game-image stubs as well.
